# Mentorship

I take free 1:1 sessions on [ADPList](https://adplist.org/mentors/vaquar-khan). Thirty to forty-five minutes. English, Hindi, or Urdu.

I can talk through a chapter, a boundary score, a migration plan, or a citation. I will not invent production metrics or grant letters for work I have not seen.

## Before you book

Come with one problem and the constraint that makes it hard. A diagram helps. A score you do not trust helps more.

Useful questions:

- Does this boundary earn a remote hop, or is it a module?
- How do I roll RVx out as a tool before it is a gate?
- What does Chapter 21 actually let me claim about cost?
